Exploring the Akan Naming Tradition

Akan Naming system. A traditional cultural practice from the Akan people of Ghana. Here a child is given a name based on the week that they were born, along with their gender.It is a deep rooted tradition that remains widely used in Ghana today.Each name carries meaning and history.
